ARRAYS - MEDIUM REVISION THEORY

Kadane's algorithm
Track the best subarray ending at the current index and the best answer seen. The local recurrence is current=max(value,current+value). Reset or restart when the old sum hurts the next segment. Handle all-negative arrays by initializing from the first element.

Rearrangement and permutations
Rearrange by sign with two output positions or an in-place strategy. Next permutation finds the longest non-increasing suffix, swaps the right pivot with the next larger value, then reverses the suffix.

Matrix problems
For spiral traversal, maintain top, bottom, left, and right boundaries and guard the final row/column to avoid duplicates. For set-zeroes, record whether the first row/column contain zero before using them as markers. Rotate a square matrix by transpose followed by reversing rows.

Majority and leaders
Boyer-Moore finds a possible majority candidate, which must be verified. Leaders are values greater than every value to their right, so scan from the right while tracking a suffix maximum.

Always distinguish subarray, subsequence, and subset; they have different constraints and solutions.
