RECURSION - GET A STRONG HOLD REVISION THEORY

This section builds recursion fluency through state, base cases, and backtracking. Each call must reduce the unresolved work or move an index toward termination.

Power
For exponentiation, repeated multiplication is O(n); exponentiation by squaring reduces it to O(log n). Negative exponents require a reciprocal and a wide numeric type.

Stack sorting and reversal
Use the call stack to remove one item, solve the smaller stack, then insert or place the removed item in its correct position. The recursive depth is O(n); sorting a stack by insertion is commonly O(n^2).

String to integer
Process digits left to right with value=value*10+digit. Validate sign, non-digit characters, and overflow before updating.

Good-number counting and combinations
Define the state precisely, avoid leading-zero ambiguity, and prune branches that cannot satisfy the constraints. Backtracking means choose, recurse, and undo the choice.
