PYTHON LIBRARIES PART 1

Built-ins useful for DSA:
list: dynamic array; append and pop at end are amortized O(1), front insertion is O(n).
dict: hash map with average O(1) lookup/update.
set: unique hashable values with average O(1) membership.
deque from collections: O(1) operations at both ends.
Counter from collections: frequency counting.
defaultdict: convenient grouped accumulation.

Useful functions: len, range, enumerate, zip, sorted, sum, min, max, any, all. Prefer clear built-ins when they express the algorithm, but still state their complexity.
